- (decision by claude) 2026-09-09 KEEP `guild` (operator, fleet-readiness-decision-batch-2026-09-09 § 2.1 row guild-B1). The preceding question is CLOSED as decided, and §5 gains the reason. **The smell is real but points the other way: `guild` does not name the artifact, it names the ABSENCE OF A STATION — the one legal way for a Dream to have no accountable Smith.** That is a job no other value does, so CAP-4 is satisfied, not strained. Supporting grounds: (a) the Lexicon already gives Guild a distinct job — "the unit of body … so the SDLC has one accountable organization, not eight freelancers" — and explicitly decouples the body from its roster ("the Guild persisted while its membership changed (six → eight) … Bodies outlive rosters"), so a body of one document is no more incoherent than a body of eight Smiths. (d) Forty days `(open)` with no consequence is itself evidence the value costs nothing. Rejected alternative: retire `guild` for `owner: charter` or a `constitutive: true` flag beside a nulled owner — weaker on both axes, since it needs a new frontmatter key (a Tier-0 contract change touching every Dream) to express what one existing enum value already expresses, and trades a noun with a job for a boolean with none. **Owed on re-derive: §5 states the reason (`guild` = the absence of a station), and `open_questions:` records the closure rather than an empty key.**
