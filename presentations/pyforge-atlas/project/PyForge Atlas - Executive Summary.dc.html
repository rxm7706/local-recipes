cf_atlas is the intelligence layer of the packaging factory. The migration re-shaped a 10k-LOC procedural orchestrator into declarative Kedro dataflow — 23 phases as pure nodes in seven fixed pipelines over a declared Data Catalog — built wave-by-wave by an agent workforce under graduated autonomy, gates first, and shipped whole.

The node shape
Declare, don't hand-wire

Declare the dataset, write the pure node, contract it — checkpointing, IO, timeouts and lineage are inherited. New signals B8–B10 landed exactly this way, zero checkpoint code.

The gates
Autonomy never bought with rigor

Frozen exit codes, six deterministic gates — each a wave's first deliverable. ~21 of 32 stories ran loop-drivable, and no gate was ever weakened to raise that share.

The read surface
Feeds beat pages

One semantic interface (the BSL) feeds every consumer — Vizro, Vizro-AI, MCP, A2A, CLI — plus a DuckDB query surface, a universe SBOM, and agent-legible feeds.
