01

The problem

The legacy orchestrator demonstrably shipped — but every phase re-implemented checkpointing, TTL gating and backoff by hand, lineage was implicit, and execution was observable only via stdout. It landed hardest on the factory's real workforce: agents cannot safely extend a 10,000-line procedural monolith.

02

Before and after

Legacy — procedural monolith

~10k LOC; per-phase state tables; one 1800s cap silently dropping phases; credentials injected on every request; lineage in the developer's head.

Migrated — declared DAG

Pure nodes (DataFrame in → out); catalog-owned IO with per-host credentials; per-node timeouts; incremental TTL re-materialization over DuckDB/Parquet.
