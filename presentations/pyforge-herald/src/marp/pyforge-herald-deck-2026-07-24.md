# capture the dream.<br>proclaim the release.

Herald is the **visual media and communications engine** of the Dream-to-Code factory — the first to touch the Dream and the last voice in the pipeline. Its first organ, the **Design↔Code bridge**, is already real.

| Role | Motto | First organ | Owns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voice + design surface | "Capture the dream. Illustrate the telemetry. Proclaim the release." | the bridge (realized) | decks · briefs · proclamations |

<!-- _class: dark -->

## Act I

# The Proclaimer

Herald **bookends the pipeline** — it renders the Dream into The Deck before the factory spins, and returns as the megaphone after the crew ships.

---

## Invisible engineering is failed engineering

Herald rejects dry, unreadable raw logs — pipeline telemetry becomes **scannable, visually striking dashboards and briefings** that reflect the original vision.

---

## Proactive synthesis

Herald is a **story-driven synthesizer**: it pulls data from the rest of the crew and stitches fragmented changelogs, audit metrics and build statuses into **one cohesive narrative**.

---

<!-- _class: dark -->

## Act II

# The organs

Herald is not all aspiration — its first organ, the **Design↔Code bridge**, is realized and documented.

---

## The Design–Code bridge — realized

Claude Design and Claude Code as **one continuous surface** (realized 2026-07-23):

- **seed** a Design project from the repo — Modernist-bound, runtime included
- **design** visually in Claude Design
- **pull** the prototype back — extract → build → export, **zero downloads**
- **etags** catch conflicts; the loop is specced as the **herald CLI** (5 CAPs)

Piloted with the Marshal deck; the whole 10-deck family round-trips through it.

---

<!-- _class: dark -->

## Act III

# The reach

One voice carried to every surface and every audience — the machinery belongs to Marshal; **Herald carries the story**.

---

## The communication face

The multiproject machinery and cross-agent portability live with **Marshal** (2026-07-23 ownership review) — Herald keeps their **communication face**: the docs, the briefs, the outward story of a method that runs on Devin, Copilot, Claude or Cursor without vendor lock.

---

## The megaphone

After the crew ships, Herald **harvests the telemetry** — Atlas and Warden outputs become infographics, commit deltas become weekly briefs, and the notables broadcast to every channel: Slack, email, wikis.

---

## The creed

# Make the factory seen.

Invisible engineering is failed engineering. Herald exists so the dream is **illustrated before the work**, and the work is **proclaimed after the ship**.
