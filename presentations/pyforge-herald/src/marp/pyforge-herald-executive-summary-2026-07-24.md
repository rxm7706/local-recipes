# Capture the dream. Proclaim the release.

### Invisible engineering is failed engineering.

Herald bookends the pipeline: first to touch a Dream — rendering it into the vision deck that aligns humans before the factory spins — and last to speak, turning run telemetry into imagery and proclamations worth reading. Its first organ, the **Design↔Code bridge**, is realized: Claude Design and the repo as one continuous surface, zero manual transfers.

---

## Why it matters — three outcomes

**Alignment before commitment**
A Dream becomes a deck in hours — cheap visual alignment before expensive build commitment. Ten decks now share one Modernist system and present side-by-side as one family.

**One continuous design surface**
Seed → design visually → pull → extract → build → export. The bridge round-trips every deck with etag-checked fidelity; the loop is specced as the **herald CLI** (5 capabilities, zero open questions).

**The story after the ship**
Telemetry becomes infographics; commit deltas become briefs; notables broadcast where the audience lives. The factory is seen — before and after.
