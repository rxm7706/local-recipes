03

Three layers, three swap points

The rule

Surfaces consume the Pipeline API only; renderers consume engine adapters only. Three swap points — LLMAdapter, PptxEngineAdapter, PlatformAdapter — and nothing else is a designed swap point.

The discipline

Outside deckcraft.engines.pptx_engine, nothing may import pptx. That is what keeps the vendor fallback clean while upstream python-pptx sits dormant at 1.0.2 (last release 2024-08-07, 534 open issues) — vendor-and-patch fires on explicit triggers, never preemptively.

The two findings on the desk

The moat moved

ppt-master: MIT, 41,032 stars and 3,406 forks in under 8 months, compiling constrained SVG into DrawingML — it meets the editability bar. It has no documented local-LLM-first or offline mode. Air-gap posture, not editability, now carries the differentiation.

One license conflict, open

pymupdf is AGPL-3.0 / Artifex commercial against a founding MIT-or-Apache-2.0-only bar. Accept with documented rationale · substitute pdfplumber · or scope the OCR fallback out. A human call, blocking Story 3.2.
