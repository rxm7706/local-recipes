04

The autonomy gradient — how far the leash goes

Autonomy here is a gradient, not a leap of faith. Personas + skills + a deterministic harness carry the agentic SDLC at every level — up to fully autonomous when a human so chooses.

L1
Assistive — AI support; the human stays the primary driver.
L2
Task-Based — the agent executes discrete tasks under explicit human approvals (per-story mode: ATTENDED).
L3
Conditional · Context Gates — the production ceiling, running here today. Unattended loops inside machine-readable boundaries; anything the agent cannot safely decide escalates to a human instead of being guessed.
L4
High-Level — the human flips from driver to governance and policy setter.
L5
Self-Governance — full autonomy; the human governs intent alone.

Live L3 evidence: 32/32 Atlas stories shipped unattended-with-gates · CRITICAL escalation via bmad-loop-resolve · a real Context-Gate escalation on 2026-07-23 · model-tier policy per story.

Personas — who decides
Human — governs intent, sets each story's autonomy level, answers Context-Gate escalations.
Marshal — the one owner of execution: every story, at every level, runs on his line.
BMAD team — Mary · John · Winston · Sally · Amelia · Paige: Marshal's sub-agents on the floor.
Crew stations — Herald · Atlas · Warden · Mason · Doctor · Scribe · Steward: independent verdicts + domain-specific skills.
Skills — the unit of execution

BMM 34+ workflows · BMB (BMad Builder) forges custom agents and workflows · skill-forge community plugins (Jira bridge) · the retro loop continuously re-forges domain skills. Everything agentic ships as a skill.

Harness — the unit of governance

bmad-loop + bmad-dev-auto: graduated gates, CRITICAL escalation, deferred-work ledger, one git worktree per concurrent loop. Deliberately not a skill — the thing that governs the agent cannot be a thing the agent authors.

"Skills are the unit of execution; the harness is the unit of governance; station verdicts stay independent. That triad is the whole agentic SDLC, at every autonomy level."
