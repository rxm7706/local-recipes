04

The autonomy gradient

L1
Assistive — AI support; the human stays the primary driver.
L2
Task-Based — discrete tasks under explicit human approvals.
L3
Conditional · Context Gates — the production ceiling, running here today. Unattended loops inside machine-readable boundaries; edge cases escalate to a human.
L4
High-Level — the human flips from driver to policy setter.
L5
Self-Governance — full autonomy; the human governs intent alone.
Personas — who decides

Human → Marshal (owns execution) → BMAD team (Mary · John · Winston · Sally · Amelia · Paige) → Guild stations (independent verdicts + domain skills).

Skills — the unit of execution

BMM 34+ workflows · BMB forges custom agents · skill-forge plugins · the retro loop re-forges domain skills.

Harness — the unit of governance

bmad-loop + bmad-dev-auto: graduated gates, CRITICAL escalation, deferred-work ledger, worktree-per-loop. Deliberately not a skill.
