GENESIS · PyForge · Dream to Code
**The founding vision + the operating-model seed**

# Everything starts with a dream.

### Build More Architect Dreams.

pyforge is a **Dream-to-Code factory**: a raw human aspiration (`docs/dreams/`, Tier 0) is rendered into a vision deck by Herald, distilled into a machine contract by BMAD, and built by a gated autonomous loop — spec-driven, never vibes. An eight-persona crew carries every Dream down one pipeline to shipped, validated code.

---

## Why it matters — three outcomes

**Deck before spec, spec before code**
Cheap visual alignment (Herald) precedes expensive commitment (Marshal + BMAD); code is built only from the contract — with gates, reviews, and escalation a human can trust.

**A crew, not a monolith**
Herald · Marshal · Atlas · Warden · Mason · Doctor · Scribe · Steward — stations own verdicts, Marshal owns the loop, and every persona has its own chapter deck in one design system.

**A seed, not just a story**
Genesis doubles as the bootstrapper: init a greenfield repo Dream-first, or adopt the tiers + crew + bridge onto a brownfield repo in place — **this repo was the first brownfield adoption**.
