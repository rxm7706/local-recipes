ACT I A craft trapped in one room
The problem and the model
01

The problem

Packaging for conda-forge is deep craft — compilers and ABI discipline, selectors, multi-outputs, per-platform matrices, reviewer conventions — and this repository already encodes it: the conda-forge-expert skill's autonomous loop, a build-failure protocol, a gotcha corpus grown one failure at a time, and an MCP server over it all. Reachable only inside a Claude Code session, in one repository, through pixi tasks in one manifest. You cannot install it. You cannot run it in CI. A colleague cannot use it. And the maintainer shipping a library still switches toolchains entirely for the conda half — a different metadata format, dependency namespace, build system, platform matrix and a volunteer review queue.

An unowned seam between two toolchains with different governance. The domain survey is unambiguous: Hatch and maturin are structurally conda-unaware, pixi publish targets conda channels with documentation silent on PyPI, and conda-smithy and the autotick-bot state in their own docs that they cannot be deployed outside conda-forge's infrastructure. That is not a missing feature in one product. Charter § Mason — dual-ecosystem mastery: PyPI and conda as complementary building blocks rather than rivals; structural integrity: deterministic, binary-compatible, reproducible — or it does not ship.
02

The seam — wrap by capability, not by product

The central decision, recorded as D-1 and enforced by test: Mason wraps the craft by subprocess for every recipe operation and builds natively for package and environment, because nothing in the wrapped machinery builds a wheel, uploads to an index or orchestrates a lock. The boundary is drawn by capability. Pure porcelain was rejected — two of the three verb families would have nothing to wrap. Extraction was rejected three times over.

Governance makes a fork adversarial. Rule 1 makes the skill authoritative over any conflicting story; Rule 2 mandates that every conda-forge effort edits the skill. A fork is continuously invalidated by the loop that governs its own domain — a gotcha added to the craft after Mason ships changes Mason's behaviour with no change to Mason.
The in-repo precedent failed. A sibling station rebuilt its orchestrator across dozens of merged stories, and the original is still the live runtime — nothing routes to the rebuild. Mason's Dream names Atlas as the cautionary tale, and Epic 5 exists to prove the seam holds with tests rather than prose.
The accepted cost, paid deliberately. Mason is not standalone: mason recipe requires a discoverable installation and is inert without one — bought in exchange for zero knowledge duplication. Self-diagnosis reports the gap before a release is attempted; it is a stated boundary, never a hidden failure.
03

The topology — the craft on the left, the engines on the right, one port between

Dependencies point inward only, and no use-case imports subprocess: that single rule stops recipe logic leaking inward one helper at a time. The craft is reached through cfe.py; every engine through one adapter protocol; the receipt leaves through one renderer.

04

Thirteen capabilities — the whole contract at a glance

The kernel is seven capabilities, each carrying a success criterion written to fail when the capability is absent and a mechanical verified: line from a re-verification at a named HEAD. Six more — the Presenton satellite — were folded in at the operator's direction as CAP-8..13, continuing the numbering and keeping their own scope. The Spec reads shipped; the Dream reads realized.

CAP-1the seam
One module is the entire boundary
no wrapped-script path outside the adapter; the root chain (flag → env → upward walk → structured degradation) and the interpreter chain each pure and unit-testable; every invocation timed, a typed error on expiry, no orphaned process; Mason reads no credential variable at all.

The dual-ship motion
wheel, sdist and conda artifact from one manifest; exactly four targets, honoured independently; a wheel-versus-conda version disagreement aborts before any upload; a missing credential is caught before anything is built; a repeat conda-forge ship reports pending and opens no second PR.
CAP-4environment
Dependency binding
solving delegated entirely to conda-lock — Mason implements no resolution logic; manifests discovered and listed before solving, explicit paths override; check exits non-zero on a stale lockfile with CI-ready JSON; runs with the craft absent.

three nouns plus top-level self-diagnosis and version; a bare noun lists its verbs and exits non-zero, with exactly one documented alias exception; under machine output stdout carries one JSON document or nothing; exit codes from one module; doctor exits zero when the non-wrapping verbs are usable and reports the gap.
CAP-6distribution
Ships the way its siblings ship
one manifest drives the conda artifact and the wheel plus sdist through the three-task build triad; engines are conda run-dependencies with version ranges mirrored in code and held in sync by a meta-test; pyforge-core is a hard dependency by a recorded cross-spec decision.
CAP-7the proof
Proving the seam holds, closing the loop
the deny-list cites every entry and ships planted violations; the sole-caller test; the independence test behind a named one-entry allow-list; the governance check green with zero implementation commits on the governed surface; the delegation-fidelity test slow-marked and clean-skipping; the effort not done until the Rule-2 retro lands.
CAP-8 · 9presenton
Air-gapped rendering · clean-room export
a bundled, air-gap-buildable Chromium with zero reachable public CDN; an editable .pptx export from source-available components replacing the opaque upstream bundle — the satellite's two rendering capabilities.
CAP-10 · 11 · 12 · 13presenton
Tiered LLM · signed image · OCP ops · drift defence
three OpenAI-compatible tiers behind one env-var contract; one pixi-locked, reproducibly built OCI image with SBOM and cosign attestation; a Helm chart on restricted-SCC defaults; a weekly upstream-drift harness whose egress never shares a runner with the air-gapped build. Parked at its Phase-0 decision gate.
Why CAP-7 is the one that makes the rest believable. This repository has already proven that intent alone does not prevent recipe knowledge from migrating into a wrapper one helper at a time. So the seam is not a convention: a deny-list whose planted-violation fixtures make an empty match a failing test; an allow-list with exactly one entry, structurally asserted; a governance check over the merged history that finds zero implementation commits touching the governed CFE surface. The counter-metrics are tracked on purpose — Mason's own line count, the recipe verb count, the adapter surface — because each one growing means the wrap decision has quietly inverted.

Persona, grammar and the skill deliberately not minted

Skills are wielded, never worn. Every other station compiles an SKF content skill from its own package; Mason is the one that does not — by policy, not by omission. Its skill tier is conda-forge-expert: a second recipe skill would be the fork the whole design exists to prevent.

Persona

bmad-agent-mason

The addressable station persona. Consults conda-forge-expert, then acts only through pyforge mason … grammar and POST /stations/mason/mcp; never freelances against the filesystem, never authors a recipe from training data. Greets, then dispatches.

The craft it wraps

conda-forge-expert

Skill version 8.90.5, gotchas numbered to 117, an autonomous loop with a success criterion per step, a build-failure protocol, a pre-PR quality gate. Authoritative over Mason on all recipe semantics under Rule 1; improved by every effort under Rule 2. Mason edits none of it.

The skill never minted

.claude/skills/pyforge-mason/

A path that must never exist — named as such in AGENTS.md and guarded by the governance-currency check. Epic 11 says it in one line: never a SKF skill that replaces conda-forge-expert. The persona uses the craft's grammar and the craft's MCP tools; no second recipe skill.

The shared floor

pyforge-core

One lattice, one envelope, one exception root: MasonError re-parents to PyforgeError, and the pyforge.core.hooks loader carries Mason's pyforge.mason.build_engine hook — rattler-build and conda-build as plugins on the shared contract, one loader, not a second.

The two doors, and only two. Path B is the unified grammar — pyforge mason recipe build …, pyforge mason package ship …, pyforge mason environment lock … — dispatched from the repo root, never a hardcoded absolute path, never the bare binary as a second public contract for personas. The service face is POST /stations/mason/mcp on the Canopy host; the /stations/mason/ portal's first job is the last diagnose, rendered through the host's PortalClient. Mason's own MCP tool surface passes the CLI-to-tool parity gate.
What the persona may never do. No recipe knowledge of its own; no replacing conda-forge-expert; no direct filesystem against recipes or source; no second PR gate; no curl, requests or WebFetch. Rule 1 in, Rule 2 out: consult the craft before any recipe code, end every conda-forge effort with the retro that improves it.

The dual-ship flow — one manifest, two ecosystems, one honest receipt

The differentiator, built natively. package build produces wheel, sdist and conda artifact from one project manifest and uploads nothing; package ship builds first if artifacts are absent by reusing that implementation, passes two gates before any upload, then attempts each target independently. The receipt tells the truth: PyPI completes in seconds, conda-forge in days behind a human queue.

The autonomy gradient — how far Mason may go on its own

Safety by default: every mutating verb accepts a dry-run flag and defaults to it where the operation is irreversible. The further a motion travels toward a public registry, the more explicit the human's confirmation must be — and beyond the upload the decision belongs to Warden, to the forge's reviewers, or to the human. Mason never grades its own work.

Dry run is the default
a PyPI upload is recognized as irreversible and the dry-run plan says so explicitly; submit forwards --dry-run to the craft; update --dry-run shows the plan without writing
Confirmation is a flag, never a prompt
--yes on ship and submit; no interactive question, so the same command is safe in CI and in a loop
Degradation is designed, never a crash
an unresolvable craft root makes recipe commands exit non-zero naming all four resolution steps and how to satisfy each; the other two families run unaffected; no traceback, ever
The one thing Mason may not decide
whether shipping was allowed — that is Warden's gate on the code and the forge's review on the recipe; Mason's receipt reports both truthfully and re-derives neither

The shipped CLI — 12 verbs under three nouns, one envelope

Console script mason, package pyforge-mason 0.1.0, run through the unified grammar pyforge mason …. Plain argparse — no CLI framework for ergonomics alone. A bare noun prints that noun's verbs and exits non-zero; --version and doctor are top-level, mirroring Warden.

noun · CFE-dependent · eight verbs

mason recipe

new --from-pypi|--from-github|--from-cran|--from-npm --output PATH
validate PATH · build PATH [--docker --config NAME]
diagnose LOG · optimize PATH · scan PATH
submit --recipe-path DIR [--yes] [--prepare-only]
update PATH [--dry-run] [--github --repo … --pre]

The lifecycle as a product face. Each verb calls exactly one adapter in cfe.py; the wrapped script's own JSON body passes through verbatim; validate is the one verb whose delegated pass/fail becomes the process exit. With no craft resolvable, every verb here exits naming the four resolution steps.

noun · CFE-independent · two verbs + the alias

mason package

build PATH [--target library]
ship --to pypi,pypi-test,conda-forge,channel:<name> [--yes] [--recipe-path DIR]
--ship TARGETS  (the one documented bare-noun alias)

Build produces wheel, sdist and .conda and uploads nothing. Ship accepts exactly the four targets and rejects anything else while listing the valid set; a pypi-test requested alongside pypi always runs first and gates it. Only the conda-forge target reaches for the craft.

noun · CFE-independent · two verbs

mason environment

lock [MANIFEST…] --output PATH [--platform linux-64,osx-arm64]
check [MANIFEST…] --lockfile PATH [--platform …]

Manifests are discovered and listed before solving; explicit paths override discovery. The engine's default platform set is reported when none is given. check exits non-zero when the lockfile is stale or the check itself failed — never a false fresh — with machine-readable output for CI.

top-level · CFE-independent · self-diagnosis

mason doctor

[--format json]  → mason_version · cfe_root + cfe_root_step

Escalation and the ledger — how a gap becomes an improvement to the craft

Behaviour Mason needs and the craft lacks is never a local patch and never a vendored copy: it is an open question routed to the craft's own retrospective. The route is fixed, each hop is owned, and the last hop lands in the one place a fork could never reach.

1 · receipt
a typed result
the wrapped script's own JSON body, or a typed MasonError with a stable identifier — never a traceback; the gap is named in the output, not hidden in a log
2 · defer
deferred-work ledger
work not taken now lands in the station's tracked deferred-work-ledger.md — the update plan-before-write finding lives there today, flagged for a story, not silently fixed
3 · route
to the craft's retro
a behaviour the craft lacks is an open question for conda-forge-expert's own Rule-2 retrospective — Mason's governed-surface rule forbids the local patch that would be faster
4 · land
the sanctioned commit
a retro: subject plus a dated changelog entry and a semver bump, its own commit, never bundled with a Mason source change — the governance check asserts exactly this shape over the merged history
5 · inherit
for free
the improvement reaches every Mason install through the port with no change to Mason — the free-inheritance verification is a story of its own, proven, not asserted

StateMeaningDrives

What each leader gets

The maintainer shipping a library

The wheel took ninety seconds and the conda half used to mean switching toolchains entirely. Now it is one sentence: mason package ship --to pypi-test,pypi,conda-forge --yes — rehearsed on the test index first, the PR opened on the forge, and a receipt that never calls a queued review "done".

The colleague who never touches a recipe

Two of the three verb families need no craft on disk at all. package build and environment lock run on a bare pixi install; doctor says exactly which verbs are unavailable and why. The tool is honest about its own edges before a release is attempted.

The conda-forge reviewer

Recipes that arrive already through the craft's own gates — validate, optimize, scan, a green linux-64 build — with finding identifiers untouched and the two-phase submission preserved. Mason adds no dialect: what the reviewer sees is what conda-forge-expert would have produced.

The platform and security lead

A tool that reads no credential variable of its own, checks upload credentials for presence before building and never logs a value; every engine a declared conda run-dependency, nothing fetched at runtime; every mutating verb dry-run by default. The attack surface is the inherited environment, and nothing else.

The skill's own maintainer

A wrapper that cannot drift into a fork: a deny-list with planted violations, a sole-caller test, a one-entry allow-list, a governance check over merged history. Every gotcha added to the skill reaches every Mason install with no change to Mason — the loop that governs the domain keeps governing it.

The executive reader

The repository's most-exercised capability, finally installable: 70/70 stories across 17/17 epics, a Spec that reads shipped, a Dream that reads realized — with the one honest caveat written down: the public publish of pyforge-mason itself is still rehearsal-tier.

Enterprise and air-gap — a builder that carries nothing in

Offline-safe and enterprise-neutral by constraint: every command not inherently requiring the network runs offline, network use is never implicit, and Mason imposes no direct-internet assumption — proxy and mirror routing arrive through the process environment, exactly as the craft's own _http.py chain (truststore, JFrog, GitHub, .netrc) expects them.

Credential blindness, confined by process. The craft's known unconditional credential-header injection stays inside the craft's own process; Mason passes the inherited environment through and reads nothing from it. A test_credential_isolation.py real-tree scan — enterprise-credential variables, banned HTTP imports, environment mutation — runs clean across the whole pyforge.mason tree.
Never downloaded at runtime. pixi, pyproject-build, twine, conda-lock and gh are conda run-dependencies discovered on PATH; a missing one is a typed error naming the engine and how to provision it. Inside an air gap that means one mirrored channel set and nothing else to allow-list.
The air-gap distribution contract has a socket. airgap_contract.py ships the contract — a mirrored channel set, a pixi bootstrap path from a bare machine with no public egress, verification hooks that prove the mirror is usable — and an empty backend registry by design, so a Miniforge/constructor or vendor installer registers and validates as a separate deliverable. Sockets, never core builds.
The craft's own enterprise rules ride through the port. PyPI sources route through pypi.org/packages/…, never the hashed CDN host that bypasses Artifactory's PyPI remote; the Python floor is read from the installed pinning, never hardcoded; format is never silently upgraded. Mason inherits every one of them without knowing any.
The satellite: Presenton, conda-native, inside the regulated enterprise. Mason is the station registered to repackage the third-party Presenton deck-generation app as an air-gapped, conda-forge-native image for OpenShift — the six capabilities folded into this Spec as CAP-8..13: a bundled air-gap-buildable Chromium with zero reachable public CDN, a clean-room editable .pptx export, three LLM tiers behind one env-var contract, one pixi-locked and cosign-attested OCI image with a CycloneDX-plus-SPDX SBOM, a Helm chart on restricted-SCC defaults, and a drift harness whose online capture never shares a runner with the air-gapped build. Its Dream stays archived at its own Phase-0 decision gate — the planning chain was consolidated here at the operator's direction, the narrative deliberately kept separate, and the contradiction with the earlier separation decision recorded rather than silently resolved. Recipe content itself remains conda-forge-expert's under Rules 1 and 2, consistent with everything above.

Integration seams — every edge, and its direction

Mason's boundaries are enforced by meta-tests, not by convention. Each seam below has a stated direction and a test — or a governance check — that fails when the direction is violated.

mason → conda-forge-expert (subprocess)

cfe.py is the sole caller: one declaration table of wrapped scripts, one adapter function per verb, list argv never a shell string, a mandatory timeout, the child killed and waited on every exit. test_adapter_sole_caller.py finds no wrapped path elsewhere; test_no_recipe_knowledge.py finds no gotcha or check code anywhere.

mason → engines (PATH)

Five engines behind one EngineAdapter protocol, each an engines/*.py module; the PyPI JSON index interrogated for idempotence. Version ranges mirrored from the member manifest and held equal by test_engine_version_range_sync.py. No engine is ever imported, only spawned.

mason ← pyforge-core

The exception root (MasonError re-parents to PyforgeError) and the pyforge.core.hooks loader carrying pyforge.mason.build_engine — rattler-build the default plugin, conda-build registered but never spawned. A hard dependency by a recorded fleet-wide decision, reconciled into the Spec.

atlas → mason (structured handoff)

The trending-candidates engine's downstream handoff: a candidate reaching packaging carries a recorded health-screen verdict and arrives as one structured record — never a recipe, never a PR; a failing or missing verdict raises with no catalog write. Atlas proposes; Mason's bench decides what to forge.

mason → warden · the forge (verdicts)

Mason's artifacts and manifests are Warden's inputs; Warden's exit is the one PR gate and Mason never re-derives or softens it. The conda-forge target's outcome is the forge's review, reported as pending with its reference until the reviewers act.

The Canopy → mason · steward's chain

pyforge mason … is the unified grammar; POST /stations/mason/mcp the service face; the /stations/mason/ portal's first job is the last diagnose. Steward's foundry cutover carries CFE comes home and Mason submits to conda-forge on its own epic, each bound to Mason's Rule 1 and Rule 2 — named in Mason's Kinships so the decomposition is not silently incomplete.
