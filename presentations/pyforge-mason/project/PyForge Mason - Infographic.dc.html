The problem

Packaging for conda-forge is deep craft: compilers and ABI discipline, selectors, multi-outputs, per-platform matrices, reviewer conventions — knowledge that lived in maintainers' heads and did not scale. Mason encodes the craft so an agent can carry a recipe from generation to merged PR, gates intact.
