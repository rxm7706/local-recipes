04 · IDENTITY
The Smiths

Eight named offices, each with a motto and a mastered craft: Herald · Marshal · Atlas · Warden · Mason · Doctor · Scribe · Steward. Three registers, one being: Smith = agent = persona.

docs/dreams/pyforge-<name>.md
A persona Dream per office; the branding law in code: PyForge Warden in prose, pyforge-warden in dists — PEP 503 makes it non-negotiable.
05 · ACCOUNTABILITY
The Stations

A lifecycle stage plus its independent verdict — the hand that builds is never the gate that judges. Two persona layers: the crew hold the stations; the BMAD team are Marshal’s sub-agents on the floor.

_bmad-output/projects/<slug>/ · .bmad-loop/policy.toml
Per-project planning artifacts and sprint status; gates and permissions in the loop policy; sub-agents configured in _bmad/custom/bmad-agent-*.toml.
06 · EXECUTION
The Skills

Wielded, never worn — the unit of execution. The harness (bmad-loop, sandbox and CI gates) is the unit of governance and deliberately not a skill: what governs the agent is never a thing the agent writes.

.claude/skills/bmad-* · _bmad/skf/
50+ installed bmad-* skills, the CFE craft, and the skill forge that re-forges them — every Rule-2 retro upgrades every future wielder.
