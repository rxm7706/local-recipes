Google's 2024 DORA report measured this across ~3,000 respondents: teams shipped more, and less reliably. The 2025 follow-up found the throughput hit had reversed — teams learned to use AI without slowing down — but the stability cost persisted. Speed came back on its own. Stability didn't, because nothing about typing faster fixes an agent making architectural decisions nobody reviewed.
