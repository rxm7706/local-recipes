Mindset 01

Preventive vigilance

A fault caught in triage

Costs a pre-flight check: a missing engine or broken config fails fast — before the factory spins, before a single sub-agent mobilizes.

A fault caught in production

Costs a mid-build failure or an outage — wasted runs, broken releases, and a debugging session that triage would have made unnecessary.

Mindset 02

Continuous pulse

Freshness

Staleness and drift — how far the shipped estate lags behind its upstreams, package by package.

Advisories

New CVEs against anything already shipped — surfaced the moment they publish, not at the next audit.

Abandonment

Upstream health — cadence collapse, maintainer loss, feedstock abandonment — caught as a signal, not a surprise.

Doctor · the Physician05 / 10
Responsibilities

The three duties

01 · Pre-flight diagnostics

Verify every required engine and toolchain is present and configured — before Marshal spins the factory.

02 · Fleet health monitoring

Track feedstock health, version staleness, upstream drift, new advisories and abandonment across the shipped estate — continuously.

03 · Remediation guidance

Translate findings into prioritized, actionable worklists — what to patch, upgrade, or retire, and in what order.

# pre-flight: is the machinery sound?
doctor check --env --engines
# continuous: watch the shipped estate
doctor monitor --fleet --watch staleness,cve
# diagnose a failure, prescribe the fix
doctor diagnose --target ./build --prescribe
