The problem

The factory had instruments everywhere and a physician nowhere: atlas health surfaces, advisory watchers, self-checks — each answering its own question, none composing a diagnosis. A fault caught in triage costs seconds; the same fault in production costs an outage (or a shipped vulnerability).

04

Consolidation, not invention

What exists

Atlas health surfaces, advisory watch, Warden's self-check pattern — live, proven, scattered.

What Doctor adds

One chart, one triage, one voice — to Marshal's line before runs, to Herald's briefs after ships, to Steward when the fault is the estate's.

The creed
Check the vitals. Diagnose the fault. Keep the ecosystem alive.
Pre-flight before the run. Pulse after the ship. Prescriptions with root causes — and a triage order the whole crew can execute.
