Responsibilities

The three duties

01 · Pre-flight diagnostics

Verify every required engine and toolchain is present and configured — before Marshal spins the factory.

02 · Fleet health monitoring

Track feedstock health, version staleness, upstream drift, new advisories and abandonment across the shipped estate — continuously.

03 · Remediation guidance

Translate findings into prioritized, actionable worklists — what to patch, upgrade, or retire, and in what order.

# pre-flight: is the machinery sound?
doctor check --env --engines
# continuous: watch the shipped estate
doctor monitor --fleet --watch staleness,cve
# diagnose a failure, prescribe the fix
doctor diagnose --target ./build --prescribe
