Mindset 01

No privilege outlives its deployment

Privilege drift, unchecked

Agents and services keep broad access long after the job ends. The live case: a registry API key that attaches to every outbound request, regardless of host.

Steward's key lifecycle

Issue scoped, rotate on schedule, revoke on teardown — audited for drift. Doctor finds; Steward remediates and owns the keys from then on.

Mindset 02

Budgets are governance

Ceilings

Machine-readable resource limits — "infra locked at $1,500/month" is a rule the system enforces, not a wish.

Alerts

Breaches surface before the invoice does — budget state is telemetry, feeding the same dashboards.

Autonomy-safe

Unattended loops spend inside the ceiling by construction — the Taxonomy view's resource governance, operationalized.

The station

The four duties

01 · Provision

Runners, CI images, pixi environments — engines present before Doctor's pre-flight ever runs.

02 · Deploy

Services, not just artifacts — the Pages console, Presenton on OpenShift, air-gap bundle installs.

03 · Hold the keys

Issue scoped, rotate, revoke, audit for drift — the credential lifecycle, owned.

04 · Enforce budgets

Machine-readable ceilings with alerts — autonomy spends inside the line.

# provision a runner for the factory line
steward provision --runner bmad-loop
# deploy a service to its platform
steward deploy presenton --target openshift
# the key lifecycle
steward keys audit --drift
steward keys rotate --scope jfrog
# the ceiling
steward budget enforce --cap 1500usd/month
