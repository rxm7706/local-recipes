The station

The four duties

01 · Provision

Runners, CI images, pixi environments — engines present before Doctor's pre-flight ever runs.

02 · Deploy

Services, not just artifacts — the Pages console, Presenton on OpenShift, air-gap bundle installs.

03 · Hold the keys

Issue scoped, rotate, revoke, audit for drift — the credential lifecycle, owned.

04 · Enforce budgets

Machine-readable ceilings with alerts — autonomy spends inside the line.

# provision a runner for the factory line
steward provision --runner bmad-loop
# deploy a service to its platform
steward deploy presenton --target openshift
# the key lifecycle
steward keys audit --drift
steward keys rotate --scope jfrog
# the ceiling
steward budget enforce --cap 1500usd/month
