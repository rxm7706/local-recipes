## Scene 02 — Act I — The Chronicler

Act I: who Scribe is. The eighth chair existed before anyone sat in it — the knowledge station was unowned until the 2026-07-23 audit.

## Scene 03 — Knowledge is lossy

The disease, diagnosed by the Sentinel ancestor in April 2026: an engineer touches six tools in the first hour; the knowledge the team runs on is scattered across them, a dozen threads, and whoever was on call. Every handoff drops context. AI without the graph hallucinates — hallucination is a context problem, not a model problem.

## Scene 04 — The compile loop

The cure, in Karpathy's words: index sources into raw, then use an LLM to incrementally compile a wiki — markdown files with summaries, backlinks, concepts, articles. Scribe industrializes that personal loop for a team: capture, curate, compile, answer.

## Scene 06 — What Scribe owns

The two dreams in Scribe's care: team-memory — the shared .claude/memory layer so what the team knows, every agent knows — and Sentinel's unbuilt core, the knowledge graph compiled nightly from real tools. Plus the curation surfaces: the Dreams index, ADRs, wikis, catalogs. The CLI cadence shows the shape.

## Scene 07 — Act III — Already beating

Act III: the Scribe habit already exists in the factory — it just didn't have a name.

## Scene 08 — Scribe-shaped machinery

Three working proofs: the append-only memlog discipline where derived artifacts re-render from the log; the personal auto-memory pipeline with thirty-plus curated entries — the single-operator prototype of team memory; and the Dream realization logs — decisions recorded where the aspiration lives. Plus the atlas Wave-H wiki crews as compile-loop machinery.

## Scene 09 — Memory that stays true

The differentiator: curation. A memory that only accumulates becomes a landfill that misleads. Scribe supersedes, dedups, and links — and a wrong memory gets corrected at the source, the way the atlas sprint-status grep artifacts were.

Close on the promise: what the team knows, every agent knows. The graph is the product; the Scribe keeps it.
