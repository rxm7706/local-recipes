# The memory
## Scribe · The Chronicler — at a glance

Capture → curate → compile → **answer**. The graph is the product.

---

## Where decisions live today

| Record | Discipline | Who reads it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **memlogs** (`.memlog.md`) | append-only; artifacts re-render from the log | bmad-spec, downstream skills |
| **Dream realization logs** | dated entries where the aspiration lives | the board, the crew |
| **auto-memory** | curated entries, superseded not overwritten | every agent session |
| **CHANGELOGs + retros** | Rule-2: every effort lands its lessons | the next effort |

---

## The compile loop (Sentinel's inheritance)

**index** sources into raw → **compile** incrementally into a wiki (summaries · backlinks · concepts) → **curate** (supersede, dedup, link) → **answer** from memory.

An engineer touches six tools in their first hour — the graph is where those six agree.

---

## Proof the habit already beats

**30+** curated auto-memory entries · **10+** spec-kernel memlogs · **25** Dreams with realization logs · derived artifacts that are **never hand-patched** — re-rendered from the record.

---

<!-- _class: dark -->

## The differentiator

A memory that only accumulates becomes a landfill that misleads. **Curation is the job**: supersede, dedup, link — correct at the source.
