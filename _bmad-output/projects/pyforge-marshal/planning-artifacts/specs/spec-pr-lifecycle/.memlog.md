- (decision) Source: docs/dreams/pr-lifecycle.md (owner marshal, status dreamt). Why: bmad-loop drives a story to a merged commit on its station branch and stops -- everything after (open PR, know which labels this repo demands, wait for checks, merge, delete branch, resync) is hand-driven or improvised from memory each time. A factory unattended until the interesting part is not unattended. Evidence, 2026-07-31: one session hand-drove five PRs (#170-174), each repeating create --repo + maintenance label + conditional environment.yaml regen + poll linter + squash-merge + delete branch + resync -- every step a written rule enforced by nothing but attention. #170 merged with a broken detector (changed a file governed by spec-pyforge-genesis without moving its memlog) because the only check that ran was the inherited linter. Merge strategy is load-bearing and invisible: squash-merging once made Epic 10's story commits unreachable from main and froze a dashboard at 36/38 with a ticking clock on a finished story.
- (decision) Cross-reference verified 2026-08-01: this Dream already resolved Open Question 10 in spec-pyforge-marshal's SPEC.md (STRUCK the PR-lifecycle non-goal, added CAP-9 the last mile lands itself, both dated 2026-07-31 -- already landed, part of PR #179, not this session's work). CAP-9 there is the compressed, ratified acknowledgment; this Spec is the detailed decomposition beneath it -- the atomic per-Dream contract Charter mandates, at the finer grain an epics/stories breakdown needs. Listed as an adopted companion so a downstream reader sees both altitudes.
- (capability) CAP-1 landing policy surface. Intent: required checks, merge strategy, label rules (including repo-specific ones -- this fork's maintenance label and its UNGATED environment.yaml sync trigger, which the label does not suppress), and branch-delete behaviour are declared once in EffectivePolicy with per-key provenance, the same treatment gates already got (marshal config). Success: a repo states its landing rules as policy; marshal land executes them with no hand-typed --repo flags, no memorized label rules, no manually-triggered environment.yaml regen.
- (capability) CAP-2 marshal land <story>. Intent: one verb performs open-or-update PR, apply labels, wait on required checks, merge, retire the branch, resync -- idempotent and re-entrant, because the interesting failure mode is a half-landed story (PR open, checks green, merge never issued). Success: re-running marshal land on a half-landed story converges to fully landed rather than erroring or duplicating the PR; the five hand-driven 2026-07-31 PRs would have required one marshal land call each instead of the six-step manual sequence.
- (capability) CAP-3 refusal semantics, mirroring teardown. Intent: landing refuses exactly as story 1.8's teardown does -- no merge on a red required check, no merge past an unacknowledged advisory finding, no silent force -- because a destructive/irreversible step (a merge to main) must not proceed on an unproven claim of green.
- (capability) CAP-4 a verdict and paper trail for the last mile (the audit triad applied to landing -- [[fidelity-enforcement]] CAP-6: Marshal builds, Doctor judges, Scribe records). Intent: every landing leaves a journal verdict recording which checks were required, which passed, what merged, and under whose authority -- closing the one place in the factory's whole claim (every stage has a supervisor, a journal, a verdict) that today has none of the three. Success: a landed story's journal entry answers, without re-deriving from git history, exactly what gated its merge and who or what authorized it.
- (constraint) Wrap, never absorb, carried forward unchanged. bmad-loop deliberately leaves this gap open -- it owns dev/verify/review/commit and stops at a station-branch commit. Marshal fills the gap AROUND the engine, in the supervisor, exactly as it already does for provisioning and teardown; landing is not a new engine stage.
- (constraint) The merge-subject contract (already Marshal's, story 1.2) is a dependency this Spec consumes, not one it redefines. Landing must preserve the existing recorded-subject contract rather than introduce a second convention -- the Epic 10 squash-merge incident is the proof of what happens when a merge step and the subject contract disagree.
- (constraint) Repo-specific landing rules (the maintenance label, the ungated environment.yaml sync check) are DATA in the policy layer, not hard-coded assumptions -- they are true of this fork specifically (staged-recipes-derived linter) and documented in CLAUDE.md precisely because they are easy to forget; CAP-1 must not bake fork-specific behaviour into code that a different repo's policy could not override.
- (constraint) Required-checks decisions must account for today's CI being advisory, not blocking ([[fidelity-enforcement]] CAP-1/constraint: findings warn, they do not block). CAP-3's refusal semantics apply to Marshal's OWN required-check evaluation, which must not assume an advisory CI green means actually green -- the gap between merged green and actually green is exactly what let #170 through, and this Spec exists to close it at the landing layer even where CI itself has not yet been upgraded.
- (non-goal) Not a redefinition of the merge-subject contract -- that stays story 1.2 / spec-pyforge-marshal's, consumed here, not re-specified.
- (non-goal) Not the engine's dev/verify/review/commit stages -- bmad-loop keeps them; this Spec starts at a merged station-branch commit and ends at main.
- (non-goal) Not Marshal's Epic 4 (landing with a durable paper trail) taken whole -- that epic is about the RECORD a landing leaves; this Spec is about PERFORMING the landing at all. They meet at CAP-4 and must not merge scope: a paper trail is worthless if the act it records is still manual, and CAP-4 here is the paper-trail obligation specifically for the act CAP-1..3 perform, not a restatement of Epic 4's full scope.
- (note) Kinships: pyforge-marshal (CAP-9 is the ratified, compressed acknowledgment this Spec decomposes), durable-runs (both are about work surviving the gap between stages -- that one saves it, this one lands it), fidelity-enforcement (the required-checks decision is a gate question; today's advisory CI is why merged green and actually green can differ -- CAP-3/CAP-4 here), pyforge-doctor (holds the verdict on Marshal's own rows), pyforge-charter.
