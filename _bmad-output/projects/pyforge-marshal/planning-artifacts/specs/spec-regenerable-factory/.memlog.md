- (event) Fifth detector added 2026-07-30: `dashboard-drift-check` (`scripts/dashboard_drift_check.py`). It closes the two ways the committed `docs/dashboard/data.js` goes silently wrong, both observed live on 2026-07-29/30 and both invisible to `dashboard-check`, which proves the board's JavaScript *runs* rather than that its contents are current. **(1) `stale-done`** — `generate.py --source git` only ever UPGRADES a story to done, so state it cannot derive from history stays at the committed baseline. That is the mechanism that lets curated in-flight state survive a deploy, and it is also how a WRONG state survives forever: squash-merging PR #132 made Epic 10's `Merge bmad-loop/<run>/10-N-…` subjects non-ancestors of `main`, and #132's own squash subject matched none of the four recognised conventions, so the published board sat at 36/38 with a ticking wall clock on a finished story for a day and nothing complained. The check compares every board status against the project's sprint feed. **(2) `missing-story`** — `scan_projects` refuses to overwrite a line whose story headings it cannot parse, keeping the hand-authored entry, so a newly added story can be silently absent from the board.
- (assumption) The detector parses `epics.md` story headings for **comparison only, never derivation**, and that restraint is deliberate. Atlas's story ids are heterogeneous BY DESIGN — Waves 0–H key on the leading id (`A1`) because their completion signal is a `story(A1)` commit subject, while Epic 10 keys on the parenthesised id (`10.6`) because its signal is a bmad-loop `10-6-…` merge — so each epic's ids match whatever its era's signal emits. A parser that mechanically derived one scheme would renumber the other half and zero out its done set, which is why `scan_projects`'s refuse-to-overwrite guard is CORRECT rather than a bug to fix. The check accepts either id form and stays agnostic. It is LOCAL-ONLY because the sprint feeds are gitignored Tier-3 that CI cannot read — the same asymmetry that caused the bug, so the check lives where the truth lives.
- (event) Mutation-tested rather than observed green, per this repo's own standing lesson about vacuous checks: reverting atlas's Epic 10 stories to `pending` on the board reproduced the exact live bug and produced two `stale-done` findings; appending a story heading absent from the board produced a `missing-story` finding; both restore clean. - (constraint) The unidentified check's rule is POSITIONAL, and the first cut was VACUOUS.
