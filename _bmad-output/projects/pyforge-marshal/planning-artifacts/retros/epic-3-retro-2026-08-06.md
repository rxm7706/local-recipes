# Epic 3 Retrospective: Supervised unattended runs

**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Epic:** 3 — Supervised unattended runs (8/8 stories done: 3.1–3.8)
**Facilitator:** Claude (direct synthesis, not full party-mode simulation — condensed per operator preference for lower-overhead retros)

## Summary

The operator can launch a gated run and walk away: a supervisor process catches idle strands, enforces budgets over externally-observed quantities, surfaces escalations, resumes safely, and now pushes durably at stage boundaries rather than on an arbitrary timer. All 8 stories merged to `main`; all 8 story specs promoted from gitignored Tier-3 scratch to tracked `planning-artifacts/specs/` (PRs #264–#267), closing a paper-trail gap the epic itself had been carrying since its first story landed.

## What went well

- **Pattern reuse compounded across the epic.** Story 3.7's escalation/notify-port design and Story 3.8's push/durability design both explicitly reused established idioms instead of re-deriving them: `evaluate_ceiling`'s pure-classification shape, the `to_redacted`/`json.loads` round-trip for redaction-at-capture (AD-34), and the already-journaled `set[str]` dedup pattern from deferral capture. Deferred-work churn dropped story over story as the epic matured: 20 (3.6) → 5 (3.7) → 5 (3.8).
- **Zero `intent_gap`/`bad_spec` findings across all 8 stories, every review round.** Every review finding stayed at the `patch`/`defer` level — no story ever needed a full loopback to re-derive its own contract. The specs were consistently clear enough to implement against as written.
- **3.8 was the only story in the epic not flagged `oversized`.** It was also the only story done outside bmad-loop (manual `bmad-create-story` + `bmad-quick-dev`, single-goal scoped deliberately). One data point, but a real one, on the operator's stated concern about loop-driven story specs running large.

## What was rough

- **7 of 8 stories (3.1–3.7) carry `warnings: [oversized]`** against the 900–1600 token SCOPE STANDARD — a systemic pattern across the epic, not a one-off.
- **The epic accumulated 82 deferred-work entries — 56% of this entire project's 146-entry backlog**, from one 8-story epic. Story 3.6 (budget ceilings) alone contributed 20.
- **Story 3.5 (idle strand detection) took the roughest path**: patch counts stayed high across roughly 4 review rounds (11 → 16 → 12 → 10), the most review churn of any story in the epic.

## Directly validated finding

Epic 4's own stated motivation ("13 of 31 story specs lost outright... caused by a step a human had to remember") was reproduced live during this retro's own preparation: none of Epic 3's 8 story specs had been promoted to tracked artifacts despite being merged and `done`, some for weeks. Recovered by hand (PRs #266/#267) rather than by tooling, because Epic 4's Story 4.1 (automated promotion) doesn't exist yet. This is current-day evidence the epic's motivating problem is real and still unsolved.
