## Summary

A story or wave that passes its gates now lands itself, end to end, with no step a human has to remember: specs promote automatically (4.1), a PR opens/updates under a hygiene preflight (4.4), a landing rule is declared policy instead of a memorized habit (4.7), `marshal land` takes a wave from "gates passed" to "merged, branch retired, feed resynced" unattended (4.8), the concurrent-write hazards that motivated the epic's own AD-42 decomposition are closed (4.9), and a fleet-wide sweep proposes exactly the stale branches it can prove are safe to delete (4.10) — closing the epic's own namesake problem for good. All 10 stories merged to `main` (PRs #266, #274–#282); all 10 story specs are now durably promoted to tracked `planning-artifacts/specs/` via a REAL `marshal deploy promote` run against this live repo (commit `e403e6e2`, zero findings, clean verdict) — the second live-fire proof of that command, and the first time it ran against a full 9-spec batch in one pass.

## What went well

- **The two-reviewer, no-shared-context adversarial pattern converged on the single most-severe finding independently, twice as often as not.** 6 of 10 stories (4.3, 4.4, 4.6, 4.8, 4.9, 4.10) had BOTH Blind Hunter and Edge Case Hunter land on the exact same top finding with zero coordination — and every one of those was a real, patch-worthy safety or correctness bug, not a false positive: a merge routine mutating the operator's own shared checkout (4.3), a hygiene-preflight bypass on a malformed policy layer (4.4), a PID-reuse collision corrupting cross-run reconciliation (4.6), a permanently-bypassable acknowledgement gate plus an unpinned merge TOCTOU (4.8, two top findings in one pass), a lock-sidecar file leaking into a tracked directory (4.9), and a git `force` flag wrong in exactly the direction that would have made `--execute` silently do nothing against this repo's own squash-merge convention (4.10). This is now validated at N=6 across two epics of evidence and should stay the unconditional default, never a step to skip under time pressure.
- **Reuse discipline held under real pressure to reimplement.** Nearly every story's own Design Notes name a moment where reimplementing would have been easier than reusing: 4.8 reused `_evaluate_hygiene`/`_batch_pr_title` verbatim rather than re-deriving PR logic; 4.10 reused `is_branch_merged`'s existing patch-id proof instead of writing a second merge-detection mechanism; 4.9 extended `run_refresh_feed` into a pure/wrapper split (mirroring `evaluate_gate`/`run_evaluate`) instead of a parallel implementation. The payoff of Epic 1–3's own primitive investment showed up directly as smaller, safer Epic 4 diffs.
- **A bug caught before review even started, by scrutinizing a spec's own literal wording against real behavior.** Story 4.8's Design Notes said "resync reuses `run_refresh_feed` in-process" — implemented literally, it printed a second, spurious envelope after `land`'s own (the exact failure mode the SAME Design Notes explicitly warned `run_batch_pr` reuse must avoid). Fixed by extracting `reconcile_feed` before either adversarial reviewer ever saw the diff — a reminder that literal spec compliance and correct behavior aren't always the same thing, and implementers should keep checking both.

## What was rough

- **Story 4.10 required real, original design work with no direct precedent anywhere in the prior 39 stories.** Its epics.md Surface line named a file (`adapters/git_local.py`) that doesn't exist and never will (the established adapter is `vcs_git.py`); its AC's "run concluded"/"story done with a recorded merge sha" facts had no existing read path until connected to Story 3.8's own `TaskPhaseSnapshot` fields (`.branch`/`.commit_sha`) — a genuinely deep research pass was needed before a spec could even be drafted, unlike every other Epic 4 story which had a clear predecessor to extend.
- **`epics.md`'s own Surface/AD citations drifted in at least two places this epic** (4.9's `adapters/git_local.py` naming echo, 4.10's own AC citing "FR-8" — "enumerate loop homes" — for a dry-run-by-default convention that's really NFR-6's territory). Neither blocked implementation, but both needed independent verification rather than trust, continuing a pattern flagged in earlier epics' own retros.
- **Deferred-work churn stayed real but bounded.** 9 new entries logged this epic (2 for 4.8, 2 for 4.9, 2 for 4.10, plus one each for 4.5/4.6-adjacent stories already captured in-epic) — smaller than Epic 3's 82-entry epic, consistent with Epic 2's own more moderate pace.
