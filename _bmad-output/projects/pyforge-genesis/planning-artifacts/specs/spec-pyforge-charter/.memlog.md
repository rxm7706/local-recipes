- (amendment-proposed) 2026-07-31 §5 — "THE TWO DREAMS" BECOMES ONE. Supersedes, quoted per CAP-1: "**`owner: guild` is reserved** for the two Dreams that *precede* the stations — this Charter, which constitutes the Guild, and [[pyforge-genesis]], the operating-model seed." Proposed: `owner: guild` is reserved for the ONE Dream that precedes the stations — this Charter, which constitutes the Guild. The prohibition is unchanged and now binds harder: nothing else may claim it; a SECOND `guild` is an unassigned Dream hiding behind a collective noun. Cause: [[pyforge-genesis]] folds into this Charter (see spec-pyforge-genesis memlog, same date) because two of its three declared jobs are gone — the seed split to [[genesis-installer]] on 2026-07-28, and the "master idea" it retains is this Charter's §1-8 and Lexicon restated. Not a demotion of Genesis: it is the completion of a split that removed the buildable half and left a narrative half that already had an owner. Load-bearing detail for the re-render: the retired `owner: crew` parenthetical stays, since the failure mode it records (a collective noun standing in for an unassigned owner) is exactly what a second `guild` would be.
- (amendment-proposed) 2026-07-31 §5 — THE CONSTITUTIVE PROJECT IS RENAMED, NOT RETIRED. Supersedes: "**Their chains live in `pyforge-genesis`** *(clarified 2026-07-28)* — the constitutive project ... It is the one project not named for a Smith, because the two Dreams it holds precede the Smiths." And: "**Target: nine projects** — eight Smiths plus `pyforge-genesis`." Proposed: the Charter's chain lives in `pyforge-charter`; it remains the one project not named for a Smith, because the Dream it holds precedes them; target stays NINE projects — eight Smiths plus `pyforge-charter`. Rationale: after the fold the project holds exactly one artifact of value, `spec-pyforge-charter`, and a project named `pyforge-genesis` that holds no Genesis is the identical defect just diagnosed in `genesis-installer` — a name recording where something was carved from rather than what it holds. Three alternatives were considered and rejected: (a) ASSIGN THE CHARTER TO A STATION and retire the project — rejected on structure, not taste: the forward chain is Charter -> Guild -> Smiths -> stations, so a station owning the Charter makes the authorized thing own its own authorization, and §5's "the hand that builds is never the gate that judges" has no higher station to appeal to at constitutional level; (b) A CONSTITUTIONAL TIER OUTSIDE `projects/` — rejected because it special-cases INV-2 for one artifact, and EXEMPLAR-STANDARD already records three such exceptions withdrawn with "the rule was sufficient every time", one of which invented a `pyforge-guild` project; (c) KEEP THE NAME — rejected as knowingly repeating a defect the same session diagnosed.
- (correction) 2026-07-31 "THE CHARTER NEEDS NO SPEC" IS FALSE AND MUST BE STRUCK. Supersedes, at pyforge-charter.md:675: "the Charter, being the unit of *legitimacy* rather than *contract*, needs no Spec." It is contradicted three ways at once: EXEMPLAR INV-1 exempts no status; this Spec exists; and its capabilities hold contracts NOTHING ELSE HOLDS — CAP-2 (every "enforced, not merely asserted" claim must name a detector that actually fails) and CAP-3 (`STATIONS` and `GUILD_DREAMS` agree across bmad_drift_check.py and generate.py, a constant mirrored in two files where updating one alone once made the board warn on a Dream the Charter permits). A constitution that forbids checking itself is precisely what CAP-2 was written against, so the line is not merely stale — it is the class of claim CAP-2 exists to refuse. Proposed replacement: the Charter is the unit of legitimacy rather than contract, and is nonetheless bound by a Spec — not to specify what it authorizes, but to keep its own claims honest and its constants true.
- (constraint) 2026-07-31 THE AMENDMENT SET IS ATOMIC ACROSS THREE MIRRORED CONSTANTS, and CAP-3 is the check that catches a half-application: `CONSTITUTIVE` and the `owner == "guild"` project mapping in scripts/dream_chain_check.py (lines 60 and 67), and `GUILD_DREAMS` in scripts/bmad_drift_check.py:615, which docs/dashboard/generate.py:624 mirrors with an explicit "keep the two in step" comment. Applying the rename or the fold to any subset leaves a Dream the Charter permits reported as unowned, or an owner resolving to a project that no longer exists. The mirror is the exact hazard CAP-3 names, so this amendment is also its first live exercise.
