Mason — environment lock & check

Lock your project’s mixed conda + pip requirements into one lockfile, and catch it when the lockfile goes stale.

✔ estate-reviewedverified 2026-10-19 · linux-64, osx-arm64win-64 untestedv1.2.0code-backed lane

Tasks: lock-env · check-env-drift · reproduce-env

Steps (works without an AI tool)

  1. Point conda and pip at Artifactory: check ~/.condarc has channel_alias: https://artifactory.example.com/api/conda and pip.conf has the internal index-url.
  2. List your inputs: environment.yml (conda) and requirements.txt (pip) in the project root.
  3. Create the lock: pyforge mason environment lock --conda environment.yml --pip requirements.txt → writes env.lock.
  4. Commit env.lock with the change that needed it.
  5. Check before you merge or in CI: pyforge mason environment check — exits non-zero if the manifests changed since the lock.
  6. Recreate the env anywhere from env.lock (step shown in the skill’s references/recreate.md).

Sample steps for the prototype; the real commands come from the published skill.
