- (decision) 39 FRs over 12 features over 16 CAPs. Features grouped by DELIVERY SEAM rather than by CAP number, because that is also how the epic pass must group them -- CAP-10 and CAP-11 both land in Containment, CAP-15/16 both in the agent-facing tiers, and CAP-2's build and its retirement obligation stay in one feature so the ordering constraint cannot be split across epics.
- (decision) Success metrics stated as BINARY GATES, not targets. No baseline measurement exists for this estate, so any percentage would be invented -- and an invented target propagates into an acceptance criterion nobody can evaluate. The only numbers used are thresholds already fixed externally (30s router timeout) or countable denominators (8 stations x 5 tiers = 40, 4 of 4 invariants, 0 duplicated chrome). Stated explicitly in the PRD so a reviewer does not read it as an omission.
- (decision) Four counter-metrics named, each attacking a way its primary could be gamed: portal count over portal thinness, event throughput over durability, skill count over skill usefulness, migration speed over the FR-23 gate. SM-C3 is the one that matters most -- eight shallow skills satisfy SM-5 and deliver nothing.
- (decision) MVP defined as 'smallest cut where the estate behaves as one system', not 'first demonstrable slice'. Excludes governed DDL (feedstock-gated, reopens shipped stories, no prior art) and flags (five absent feedstocks). Lane 1's BUILD may land in MVP but its REMOVAL may not -- the parity precondition is not compressible.
