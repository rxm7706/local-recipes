# EGM audit 2026-11-05 — pyforge-steward

**What this is.** The durable residue of the first automated Elephant-Goldfish (EGM) audit of the PyForge fleet, landed by marshal Story 113.18 under spec-pyforge-marshal:CAP-302. `marshal factory challenge --audit` ran on `claude-sonnet-5-5`. Fresh-context reviewers from the `egm@egm` plugin read one slice, one Dream entry and the repo standards.

**Scope.** Rules: the station's compiled skill. Capabilities: CAP-178. Epics: Epic 118. Dream entry given to reviewers: 2026-10-31 (`dream_choice: names-cap`).

**Triage.** Nine Sonnet agents re-checked all 139 high findings against `main` at `836a2ee286`. Five more tried to overturn the 73 rejections and ran a consequence check on the 22 accepts that change code, a test, a Spec, a story spec or a ledger. The operator ruled "Accept all 53" on 2026-11-05; the 68 rejections and 18 deferrals stand as recommended.

**Limits.** The first-pass brief told verifiers that reviewer severity runs loud, and all 139 highs fell to medium or low, so read the severity drop as influenced by that brief. Every agent ran Sonnet, the same model as the challenger.

**Result.** 19 high findings: 4 accepted, 10 rejected, 5 deferred. 66 medium and 26 low findings are not triaged.
