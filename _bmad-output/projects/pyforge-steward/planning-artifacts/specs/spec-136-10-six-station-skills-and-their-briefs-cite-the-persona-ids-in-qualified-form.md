## Intent

**Problem:** `DW-steward-136-4-1`. After Story 136.4 the personas cite `canopy:FR-13`, `canopy:FR-11`, `spec-pyforge-unifying-strategy:CAP-15` and `spec-pyforge-unifying-strategy:CAP-16`, but the `skill-brief.yaml` of atlas, doctor, herald, marshal, scribe and warden, and the compiled `SKILL.md` of atlas, doctor, marshal and warden, still cite one or more of the four bare.

**Approach:** For each of the six stations in turn: qualify the ids in its `skill-brief.yaml`, then regenerate the compiled skill with `skf-update-skill` against the live source and export it with `skf-export-skill`, by the route steward Story 134.3 took and the team-memory note on that run records. One station at a time, since the export manifest, the result file and the `AGENTS.md` block are shared. Earlier versioned skill directories are history and are not edited. A doctor test fixture that copies a brief is updated only if a test compares it to the live brief.
