# EGM audit 2026-11-01 — pyforge-atlas

**What this is.** The durable residue of the one-time Elephant-Goldfish (EGM) audit of the PyForge fleet, landed by marshal Story 113.7 under spec-pyforge-marshal:CAP-302. Fresh-context reviewers from the `egm@egm` 0.1.0 plugin read this station's newest CAP slice (the mint stage) and the epics that decompose it (the decompose stage). The run was done by hand on 2026-11-01 before the challenger verb existed; the operator ruled that this hand run counts as the audit.

**Scope.** Dream entries: 2026-10-18 *atlas fills its own data*; 2026-10-19 *the Vizro boards read what the pipelines write*. Capabilities: CAP-64..CAP-69. Epics: Epics 34-37.

**Triage.** Every finding was re-checked against `main` at `03a7aff5d9` (after Epic 113 merged), then put to an adversarial check: every Blocker and every accepted code, Spec or story-spec change by refute and consequence lenses, and every rejection by a refute lens. Severity was re-graded where the consequence lens showed nobody downstream acts on a false claim. The operator accepted every recommendation on 2026-11-01. Each accepted finding is one `event` line on its owning Spec's `.memlog.md`.

**Result.** 30 findings: 23 accepted, 6 rejected, 1 deferred.
