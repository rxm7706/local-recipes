(1) [HIGH] `main()` now checks `args.create_issues and not gh_bin()` right after the `--gist-only` early-exit and returns 1 with a clear stderr message, instead of letting `create_missing_issues`'s internal `SystemExit` fire deep inside `main()` after all the inventory computation — a live `--create-issues` run with no `gh` on PATH previously lost the whole run's output (`write_xlsx_tab`/`write_csv`/markdown never ran).
