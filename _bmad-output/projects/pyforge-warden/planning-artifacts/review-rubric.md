# PRD Quality Review — pyforge-warden

*Reviewed 2026-07-16 against `.claude/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`. PRD: `_bmad-output/projects/pyforge-warden/planning-artifacts/prd.md` (no addendum.md). Frozen contracts (7-rung verdict lattice, exit enum `{0,1,2,130}`, one-verb CLI) and the canonical FR1–FR40 block are treated as settled per the spec (D12, 2026-07-16), not re-litigated here.*

## Overall verdict

This is an unusually rigorous PRD: the FR1–FR40 contract is testable almost line-by-line, decisions are dated and owned, reversals are recorded rather than erased, and the "honest coverage / never false-green" thesis visibly drives journeys, FRs, NFRs, and metrics alike. What's at risk is navigational, not substantive: three stacked supersession layers (2026-07-15 replan > 2026-07-12 corrections > 2026-07-11 reconciliation) have left the consolidated Architecture Open Questions register partially stale against decisions the banners already record, and a few leftover tokens (EPSS in the deferred list, "clean at N%" in J1) contradict the freshest re-baseline. A downstream reader who trusts the banners will be fine; one who trusts a single section in isolation can mis-resolve. Fix the register and the leftovers and this PRD is build-ready.

## Decision-readiness — strong

Decisions are stated as decisions with dated labels (D1, D2, D3, D8, D12), and — rarer — reversals are stated as reversals with the original retained: "§ Cross-step decision to carry into Step 9" is explicitly marked "**[SUPERSEDED by D3 (2026-07-15) + D12 (2026-07-16) — retained as history]**", and the KEV cut-reversal in § Traceability answers the original cut "on **both** clauses." Trade-offs name what was given up ("lightweight = runtime-footprint, not total-cost (fixture-maintenance tax…)"; the cardinal-rule asymmetry "a missed CVE… is catastrophic; a blocked build… is costly but recoverable"). The § Architecture Open Questions split into "Resolved v1 assumptions (recorded — do not relitigate)" vs "Open — architect must decide" is exactly what this dimension asks for — which is why its staleness (below) is the PRD's single sharpest defect.

### Findings
- **high** Stale open-decisions register (§ Architecture Open Questions (consolidated)) — the section claims to be the "*Single source of truth for the next phase*," but the architecture phase is already complete (2026-07-11 banner: "`architecture.md`, status: complete") and resolved several of its items. Item 1 "[Gap A — sharpest]" still says "*architect must decide… Recommended default for the architect to confirm: hygiene is a **separate `warn`-axis***" — while reconciliation item 3 and canonical FR18 already decided it the *other* way ("**DEP001 (missing dependency) blocks by default**"). Item 3 "[Gap C]" says the osv input contract is "*fully deferred today*" while banner item 4 and FR13 already carry the bundled-map/`pypi_identity` resolution. Item 8(c) (waiver write-target) is answered by its own parenthetical. A reader acting on this register would reopen settled decisions or, worse, follow the stale Gap-A recommendation against FR18. *Fix:* re-stamp the section post-reconciliation — move resolved items into "Resolved v1 assumptions" with their deciding reference (banner item / FR), and keep only genuinely-open items under "architect must decide."
- **medium** EPSS listed as deferred in the section that says it isn't (§ Project Scoping › Complete Feature Set, "Nice-to-Have / Deferred to post-v1") — the list's own preamble states "*KEV, EPSS, the axis gates, baseline & grandfathering, and fix PRs moved INTO v1 and left this list*," yet the list still ends "*· better conda↔PyPI reconciliation · EPSS.*" FR36, the MVP paragraph, and the CLI section all ship `--min-epss` in v1; a reader scanning only the deferred list gets the opposite answer on a D12 headline item. *Fix:* delete the trailing "· EPSS" token.

## Substance over theater — strong

Nothing here is furniture. The persona roster runs to eight entries — above the rubric's four-persona guideline — but every one demonstrably earns its row: P7 exists to own the corpus regression gate (J8, "*a promise with no owner has no teeth*"), P6 exists solely "*to justify the durability property of FR8, not to smuggle in a retention requirement*," P8 was added with a dated rationale (waiver authoring is local-only by design), and the roster explicitly *cuts* a P5 ("*Cut from the roster… the authorization moment is captured as a waiver-schema field, not a distinct person*") — the opposite of persona theater. The Innovation section is grounded in a dated competitive spike that *narrowed* its own claim ("*caught a potential over-reach: vuln scanning of installed conda environments already exists*") and names what incumbents own ("the one-CLI / one-gate / SBOM *shape* (Trivy)"). NFRs are mechanism-worded and product-specific (AST denylist, socket-guard test, "≤ ~2s p95… measured with engines stubbed," zip-slip confinement) — no boilerplate anywhere. No findings.

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis is stated and load-bearing: "*both jobs begin at the same manifest parse*" → one front-door, independent axes, one honest exit code; the wedge (pre-build source-manifest scanning + conda hygiene, "zero incumbents") is distinguished from the byproduct (unification). Success metrics validate the thesis rather than activity — `false-green = 0` on seeded fixtures, parse coverage ≥ 98%, split-coverage fields asserted independently — and the anti-metric ("gate-disabled events → target 0") is honestly annotated as "*Not in-tool-measurable… tracked via measurable proxies*." MVP scope kind is declared ("**problem-solving MVP**") with matching scope logic.

### Findings
- **medium** The MVP framing was not re-argued after D12 grew v1 (§ Project Scoping › Strategy & Philosophy) — the strategy paragraph still describes "*the minimum that makes the beachhead (P1 conda/pixi maintainer) say 'this is useful'*" with "*the cost center is the E1 extractor… not infrastructure*," yet D12 re-baselined v1 to 6 epics / 29 stories including two whole new axes (license, currency), two new gated feeds (KEV, EPSS), a baseline ratchet, and a fix-PR actuator with forge egress (FR40 — a different risk class from a read-only scanner, acknowledged only via the NFR-S2 exception). The PRD *records* D12 everywhere but never re-derives why the enlarged v1 is still the beachhead minimum rather than scope growth by fiat. *Fix:* one paragraph in Strategy & Philosophy bounding the D12 additions against the thesis (e.g., flag-activated gates keep the beachhead's zero-config path unchanged; the actuator is inert without opt-in).

## Done-ness clarity — strong

This is where the PRD is at its best. Nearly every FR carries a testable consequence, and § Measurable Outcomes gives the executable form ("*a non-expired waiver → exit 0… an expired waiver → re-block (exit 1)*"; "*BOM component count == resolved-inventory count*"; the multi-axis DoD bullet). Vague-word candidates are consistently pinned: "best-effort" (FR5) is bounded by the supported-construct matrix + name-only+marked degrade; "lightweight" is bounded by NFR-P-warm's "≤ ~2s p95… engines stubbed… p95/median over N runs, never a single run"; even the stale-waiver matrix and offline-DB state-machine cells are enumerated. FR40 has behavioral ACs (dry-run opens nothing; "*a failed PR-open never alters the verdict*").

### Findings
- **medium** The coverage promise's own definitional artifacts are unowned (§ Architecture Open Questions › "Owners still to assign") — the PRD itself flags that the per-format supported-construct matrix ("*the thing without which coverage is undefined per format*") "*currently lives only in Risk-Mitigation prose*" with no owning FR/NFR; that `environment.yml`/`pixi.toml` are "*'sampled' with no baseline*" (so two of the six headline formats have no ratcheted conformance gate behind the "6-format bridge" claim); and that FR30's dual-config robustness ACs live only as CLI-section prose. Self-flagging is honest, but in "THE binding v1 capability contract" an unowned testable consequence is a consequence a story can skip. *Fix:* assign each — e.g., fold the construct matrix into the FR5/FR15 cluster as a named deliverable, extend NFR-R1/R2 with a (smaller) environment.yml/pixi.toml fixture baseline, and give FR30's ACs an NFR or FR home.

## Scope honesty — strong

Omissions are explicit and repeatedly boundary-marked at exactly the places a reader would otherwise assume coverage: retention ("*Retention and retrieval are the CI system's job, not the tool's*", J7); the waiver trust boundary ("*Was the approver real?… out of the tool's process boundary… and the PRD says so rather than pretending otherwise*", J4); fleet aggregation ("*a by-design non-capability, **not** a fleet-aggregation FR*"); typosquat detection ("*the tool scans **declared** deps, not resolver substitution*"); telemetry (anti-metric proxies). The formal `[ASSUMPTION]`-tag convention is absent, but "Resolved v1 assumptions (recorded — do not relitigate)" performs the same function with better ergonomics (noted in Mechanical). Open-items density is high in absolute terms (10 open architecture questions + 3 unassigned owners on a build-greenlit PRD) but is materially lower than it appears once the stale entries are removed per the Decision-readiness high finding — the genuinely-open residue (multi-manifest precedence, conda range model, dedup key, pixi runtime role) is appropriately architecture-shaped, and most already carry a recorded v1 assumption. No additional findings beyond those already filed.

## Downstream usability — adequate

The FR-label crosswalk table in § Functional Requirements ("*Requirement-ID conventions (read first)*") is the load-bearing navigation device and it works: every colliding narrative label (FR5/FR7/FR8/FR9/FR10/FR11, FR-NEW-A–F, the spec's FR-K1/L1/L2/C1/C2/B1/A1) resolves to a canonical FR, NFR aliases are mapped, and the `cf_atlas FR-16/FR-18` vs PRD-FR16/FR18 trap is explicitly disarmed. IDs FR1–FR40 are contiguous and unique. Journeys carry their implied requirements forward into a summary table that Step-9 visibly consumed. What's missing for a chain-top PRD is a glossary — this document runs on an unusually dense frozen vocabulary and currently defines it only inline at first use.

### Findings
- **medium** No glossary, and the axis/dimension terminology drifts (§ CLI Requirements, § FRs, § State-Machine Behaviors) — FR15 defines coverage "**per axis**… (v1: hygiene, vulnerability, license, currency)," but `--require-full-coverage` "*promotes any `skipped` **dimension***," the crashed-engine cell reports "*per-**dimension** status*," and J5 says "**per-axis** `coverage`." Likewise "status" / "verdict" / "rung" circulate around the same lattice. Story extraction will lean on these nouns; a 10-line glossary (axis, dimension→axis, verdict/status/rung, coverage kinds, finding vs component, gating) removes the ambiguity cheaply. *Fix:* add a Glossary section and normalize "dimension" → "axis" (or declare them synonyms there).
- **low** Live label-collision hazards, mitigated but not removed — two different decisions are both named "D3" (the spec's KEV reversal vs the CLI step's dual-config; the collision is flagged inline at "*D3 (CLI-step decision label — distinct from the spec's D3 KEV decision)*"), and narrative "FR8/FR9/FR10" mean SBOM/waiver/typed-errors while canonical FR8/FR9/FR10 mean hygiene-findings/deptry-ignores/vuln-findings. The crosswalk covers a reader who read the conventions block; a reader landing mid-document (e.g., J4's "FR9 waivers-as-code") can still mis-resolve. *Fix:* mechanically rewrite narrative FR labels to canonical IDs (or to neutral names like "the waiver FR"), and rename the CLI-step decision D3→D3-CLI.
- **low** J1's climax leads with a superseded rendering (§ Journey 1) — "*it renders **`clean at 60% coverage`***" survives from before the `indeterminate` state, and FR16 explicitly retires that phrasing ("*The earlier 'clean at N%' phrasing predated `indeterminate` — corrected 2026-07-12*"). J1 does append the correction two sentences later, but the stale token is the bolded, quotable one. *Fix:* reword to the qualified-verdict form (e.g., "`indeterminate` — 60% covered, 40% unresolved").

## Shape fit — strong

The shape matches the product: a capability-spec PRD for a non-interactive CI gate, with CSV-guided CLI-tool sections doing real work (command structure, config precedence with parsing ACs, streams discipline, the `_engine_env()` normalization helper, contract-stability taxonomy) and the human-UI sections explicitly skipped with reasons ("*visual_design / ux_principles / touch_interactions skipped — N/A*"; "*Accessibility is skipped (no human-UI surface)*"). Ten journeys is high density for a CLI tool, but this is not over-formalization: each journey exists to surface a requirement cluster (J8 is the test strategy given an owner, J9 is the verdict-composition contract, J5 is the machine consumer), and the Journey Requirements Summary proves they were consumed. Treating the pipeline (M1) as a first-class persona is exactly right for a tool whose "primary consumer is a pipeline, not a human terminal." Brownfield-adjacent references (the ~1,950-file recipe corpus, `docs/enterprise-deployment.md`, the committed scaffold path) are specific and checkable. No findings.
