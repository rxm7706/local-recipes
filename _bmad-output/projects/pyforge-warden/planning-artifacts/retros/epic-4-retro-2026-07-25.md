## Delivery snapshot

The machine-contract surface: **CycloneDX SBOM emission (4.1)** — a one-story epic. Warden can emit a CycloneDX bill-of-materials as an independent sibling artifact alongside its compliance verdict. **1/1 done, merged via PR #104.**

## What went well

- **The SBOM is an independent sibling artifact, not entangled with the verdict.** Its rendering and success are decoupled from the pass/fail decision — an SBOM-emission failure never changes the exit code. This clean separation is the same discipline later applied to 6.9's actuation payload (side-effect, never influences the verdict).
- **It extends the machine contract without touching the frozen core** — the `ComplianceReport` schema (frozen since 1.1) was untouched; the SBOM rides alongside.
- **A single, well-scoped story epic is a feature, not a gap** — one deliverable, one clean landing, no review-cap drama.

## Lessons

1. The "independent sibling artifact" pattern established here (SBOM decoupled from verdict) became a reusable principle — reapplied deliberately in Epic 6.9 (the fix-PR actuation payload flows only into a pass-through slot, never the verdict).
2. Small epics don't need ceremony; the retro's value here is mostly recording the decoupling principle for reuse.
