**What this is.** The durable residue of the one-time Elephant-Goldfish (EGM) audit of the PyForge fleet, landed by marshal Story 113.7 under spec-pyforge-marshal:CAP-302. **Scope.** Dream entries: 2026-10-12 (two entries), 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14 entries. Capabilities: CAP-58..CAP-61. Epics: Epics 39-43.
