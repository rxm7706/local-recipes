# Glossary — Scribe

Companion to `SPEC.md`, pointer-only: one line per station term the Spec uses that the 2026-11-01 EGM audit's
reviewers found undefined (`../../research/egm-audit-2026-11-01.md`), each saying where the term is defined. Fleet
terms (station, `CAP-n`, `FR-n`, `AD-n`, the tiers, Path B, the `(ready <date>)` and `(shipped <date>)` markers) are in
`docs/governance/spec-one-chain-per-station/CHAIN-STANDARD.md` § 0, § 2 and § 4. The cross-harness facts behind CAP-27
and CAP-29 are in `../../research/multi-harness-instruction-surface-2026-09-19.md` (§ 7 for the `agents-md` mod); the
decisions behind every CAP are in this folder's `.memlog.md`. Below, "the parity meta-test" is
`src/shared/packages/pyforge-scribe/tests/meta/test_instruction_surface_parity.py` and `catalog.py` is
`src/shared/packages/pyforge-scribe/src/pyforge/scribe/catalog.py`.

## Instruction surface (CAP-27, CAP-29, CAP-30)

- **Per-tool file, pointer file** — a harness's own file beside `AGENTS.md` (CAP-27's intent lists them); the 60-line rule reads the parity meta-test's `POINTER_FILES`, the duplicate-H2 guard its `DUPLICATE_GUARD_FILES`.
- **Harness table** — the table in `AGENTS.md` § *How each harness loads this file*.
- **Import floor** — `CLAUDE.md`'s bare `@AGENTS.md` line (research § 7, first row).
- **The mod, the pinned mode** — Claude Code's built-in `agents-md` mod and its `instructionFiles` mode `claude-md-and-agents-md` (research § 7).
- **Atlas child** — the nested `src/shared/packages/pyforge-atlas/AGENTS.md` (Dream § Realization log, the 2026-09-20 bullet).
- **Spelling normalisation** — the parity meta-test's `_normalise_heading` (its comment states the rules).
- **Runtime scope** — `DETECTOR = {"scope": "runtime"}`, defined in the `scripts/detectors.py` docstring.
- **`bmad:context` block, managed block** — the `AGENTS.md` lines between `<!-- bmad:context -->` and `<!-- /bmad:context -->`, owned by `.claude/skills/bmad-project-context/SKILL.md` (Constraint *Managed block and per-tool files*).
- **SKF managed block** — the separate `<!-- SKF:BEGIN … -->` / `<!-- SKF:END -->` block that `.claude/skills/skf-export-skill/SKILL.md` writes into the files `_bmad/skf/config.yaml` `ides` names.
- **The 2026-09-26 end state** — the three asks of the Dream § 2026-09-26, checked by the parity meta-test's `_skf_end_state_breaks`.
- **Aspiration** — in CAP-30, a managed-block line the parity meta-test's `_ASPIRATION` matches.
- **Typed count** — a count in the managed block the parity meta-test's `_TYPED_COUNT` matches.
- **Runtime mode check** — `scripts/claude_instruction_mode_check.py` (pixi task `claude-instruction-mode-check`); its docstring states the exit codes.
- **`pyforge-scribe-test`** — the station suite, `pixi.toml` `[feature.pyforge-scribe.tasks.pyforge-scribe-test]`.
- **The dispatch-launch half** — `spec-pyforge-marshal:CAP-262` (marshal Story 46.11).

## Local cluster (CAP-31)

- **scribe-pg cluster** — what `scripts/scribe_pg.py` manages through the pixi tasks `scribe-pg-up`, `scribe-pg-down` and `scribe-pg-status` (`-e pyforge-scribe-pg`).
- **Socket dir** — the directory `_socket_dir()` in `scripts/scribe_pg.py` chooses (its docstring states the order).
- **Length guard** — `SOCKET_PATH_MAX` and `_prepare_socket_dir()` in `scripts/scribe_pg.py`.
- **Reuse** — `up()` in `scripts/scribe_pg.py` using a server already listening on 127.0.0.1:5433 (the comment above its `_is_up()` call).

## Estate catalog (CAP-32)

- **Estate catalog** — `docs/reference/bmad-estate-llms-full.md`, generated by `scribe catalog bmad-estate` (its header names the regeneration command and the detector).
- **Derived facts, section digest, prose** — `Estate.section_facts()`, `digests()` and `_DIGEST_RE` in `catalog.py`; its module docstring says what is exempt (OQ-CAP-32-1, decided in the memlog 2026-09-29).
- **Section sources ("traceable")** — `SECTION_SOURCES` in `catalog.py`.
- **Skill family** — the `FAMILY_*` constants and `_family_for()` in `catalog.py`.
- **Verdict, wielder, provisioning path, hazards** — columns of § 1 of `_bmad-output/projects/pyforge-steward/planning-artifacts/specs/spec-pyforge-steward/adoption-register.md`.
- **Disagreement** — `find_disagreements()` in `catalog.py` (Constraint *The catalog derives, never decides*).
- **`installShims`, `skf-manifest.yaml`** — `_bmad/_config/manifest.yaml` and `_bmad/_config/skf-manifest.yaml`, read by `read_installed()` in `catalog.py`.
- **`HARNESS_VERSION_RANGE_TEXT`** — marshal's constant in `src/shared/packages/pyforge-marshal/src/pyforge/marshal/adapters/harness_bmadloop.py`, read by `read_harness_range()` in `catalog.py`.
- **Release-cadence step headings** — `_bmad-output/projects/pyforge-steward/planning-artifacts/specs/spec-pyforge-steward/release-cadence.md`, read by `read_cadence_steps()` in `catalog.py`.
- **pipeline-truth** — the output of `steward suite pipeline-truth --json`, read by `read_pipeline_truth()` in `catalog.py`.
- **Kill switch** — the flag `pyforge.scribe.bmad_estate_catalog` in `src/platform/config/flags.json`, under `spec-feature-flag-governance:CAP-7` (`docs/governance/spec-feature-flag-governance/SPEC.md`).
- **INV-4** — in the Constraint *No station internals for a constant*, the memlog's 2026-09-29 constraint entry ("the detector is owned"); the archived `spec-fidelity-enforcement` uses INV-4 for a different invariant (`archive/_bmad-output/projects/pyforge-marshal/planning-artifacts/specs/spec-fidelity-enforcement/.memlog.md`, CAP-2).
