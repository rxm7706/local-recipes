The kept question

How might the internal agile team see every piece of work that already lives on internal Jira, the internal GitHub Project, and the external vendor GitHub Project, without anyone needing access to a board they are forbidden to open? Kept. Visibility only. We = the internal agile team, all roles at once.

This week's pain is see everything — not connect, not analytics. Same work can sit in Jira quarterly planning, on the vendor board, and on the internal board used to test vendor solutions. Postgres is not a choice; it is a constraint.

Alien anthropologist & swarm logic

Field notes from the visitor

Why keep work in rooms the others are forbidden to enter, then ask a third room to remember all the rooms?

They already have a shared External GitHub Project and still mourn a private one they cannot open. The grief is the requirement.

“See everything” means see the ritual, not the workshop. The private repo is a temple. The packet is the offering.

BMAD stories live in a git folder and they want them on a Project — they are photocopying a ledger onto a fridge magnet.

The product is a customs house at the wall, not a window into the temple.

Rules the swarm will not break

  • A tracker writes stamps only about itself.
  • If you cannot read a board, emit occluded — never invent a stamp.
  • A human may mint by pasting two URLs; the swarm does not guess a third.
  • Collectors for live boards we own; loaders for extracts we do not; no collector for their private GH.
  • Ready-to-test and stale are derived bits. Analytics never write trackers.
  • Epic 8 only when both endpoints sit on the allowed-pair list.

The extract corridor — two dated lists

Click an envelope. The air-gap is a pair of drop zones. Both land in Postgres. Neither is a live board login.

We see whatever they chose to emit, on the cadence of the drop. Their private issue numbers may arrive as nicknames; those URLs stay unclickable. Occluded shrinks to missing fields, or a late drop — not “their whole book is invisible.” Idempotent: re-dropping last week does not mint twins.

SLICE

Us → vendor

may-leave only · signed slice · they load it

Testers’ findings and shared book items. Never the full Jira Kanban. Never secrets. Never factory BMAD unless marked vendor-shared. We do not push with our credentials. Postage due: outbound without may-leave is refused at the door.

See-everything, restated after the corridor: our live Jira + our live Internal GH + last successful vendor extract + last outbound we sent them. Page title is the timestamp. High tide is inbound; between tides the beach shows stranded as-of, not empty ocean.

Rituals of the house

Drop Night

Vendor sends the file. A named receiver stamps the waybill. Standup the next morning cites the waybill — not “any updates from the vendor.” Testers only pull shipped after Drop Night. Hunting private GH is taboo.

Quarantine Hour

Twice weekly the lead links or rejects unlinked inbound rows. No auto. No title-match. Holding pen, not a merge.

Outbound Blessing

A reviewer signs the slice name before the file may leave. Unsigned file does not render. Reviewer on PTO: testers keep last signed slice.

Metamorphosis

Egg

Table in the existing Postgres app. CSV in, CSV out. As-of times. Standup view. Shipped view. No write to any tracker. Do not skip the egg. Adult BI with no trusted inbound drop is a costume.

Larva

Live collectors for Jira and Internal GH we already own. Still extract-only for vendor. Internal GH fridge. BMAD projection (cards say projected; ledger remains source). Vendor column is a view, not a clone of the temple.

Named winner if forced today: egg table + two views. Fridge and BMAD wait in the next instar. $0 already proves the corridor is a social contract and a table, not a platform buy.

MoSCoW — convergence 14 September 2026

Must

Inbound and outbound extract. Frozen tiny schema. Passport UUID. As-of and waybill. Column allowlist. Outbound slice and signer. File-only loader. No PAT into vendor org. Join-store — not a fourth Kanban.

Should

Standup and shipped views. Quarantine, no auto-link. vendor_id and batch sha. Empty file equals fail.

Could

Internal GH fridge. BMAD projection, vendor-shared only. Epic 8 inside our walls. Herald weekly book. Inventory on extract history.

Won't

PAT into the vendor org. Title-match as identity. A fourth Kanban in the app. Live sync to their private GitHub. Fabricate rows so the pane looks complete. Treat extract as live. Dump the whole Jira outbound. Unito, Exalate, a GitHub App on their side, a borrowed laptop.
